MRV Failsafe Brake

NASA Johnson Space Center · Engineering Intern

Failsafe brake assembly

Project overview

A brake prototype for NASA’s Modular Robotic Vehicle using a 220 lbf gas shock. Prototype validation supported resumption of MRV rebuild efforts.

What I did

I modeled and iterated the assembly in SolidWorks, checked packaging, and used FEA to evaluate load capacity. I created GD&T drawings for in-house and outsourced parts and machined a prototype using a manual lathe and mills with digital readouts, meeting ±0.002 in positional tolerance requirements.
