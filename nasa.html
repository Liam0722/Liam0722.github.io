MRV Failsafe Brake

NASA Johnson Space Center · Engineering Intern

Brake design and sizing

Project overview

A brake prototype for NASA’s Modular Robotic Vehicle using a 220 lbf gas shock.

Modeling and design

I used Desmos to model the vehicle’s braking-force requirements, accounting for load transfer during braking to determine the required braking force at each motor.

I modeled and iterated the assembly in SolidWorks, checked packaging, and used FEA to evaluate load capacity.

Manufacturing and testing

Prototype manufacturing

I created GD&T drawings for in-house and outsourced parts and machined a prototype using a manual lathe and mills with digital readouts, meeting ±0.002 in positional tolerance requirements.

Test result

Testing measured a braking torque of 420 ft·lbf at the motor, 5% above the design target. The additional torque likely came from the mechanical advantage of the drum brake shoes and internal friction.

Prototype validation supported resumption of MRV rebuild efforts.
