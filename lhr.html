FSAE Suspension & Sensor Enclosures

Longhorn Racing · Suspension Team Member

Suspension linkages

Project overview

Double-wishbone suspension linkages and apexes for the 2026 electric Formula SAE car. The analysis included member loads, tube sizing, buckling, stress, and stiffness.

What I did

I designed linkages and apexes and used ANSYS FEA, buckling calculations, and a custom Python truss-analysis script to evaluate and refine the members. I also developed an Excel calculator for motion ratios, ride frequencies, jacking forces, and roll rates.

Ride-height sensor enclosure

Project overview

Electronics enclosures for ride-height sensors at all four corners of the car.

What I did

I iterated the housing and used Parker’s O-Ring Handbook for seal selection and groove sizing. The enclosure passed a 10-minute immersion test and a 10-minute low-velocity water-spray test.
