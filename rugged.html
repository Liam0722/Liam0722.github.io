Ink fill station

Project overview

A desktop station for dispensing solvent-carrier ink, with an ESP32 controller and load cell for dosing measurement.

What I did

I designed the station and integrated the ESP32 and load cell, achieving ±2 mL dosing accuracy. I also iterated manifold face seals using Parker’s O-Ring Handbook to isolate the solvent system from the environment.

Other internship work

I modeled a 20-micron filter housing using surface modeling to seal the chassis against dust ingress. I also designed and manufactured 3D-printed and machined assembly jigs, supported by GD&T drawings, for robot-subsystem assembly and checkout.
