Robot Mechanisms & Team Leadership

FIRST Robotics Team 624 · Team President

Robot mechanism design

Project overview

Climb, end-effector, and deflector subsystems for FIRST Robotics competition robots.

What I did

I designed and iterated these mechanisms in Onshape and SolidWorks and helped lead robot design, build, and integration.

Team involvement

As president, I led design, build, integration, sponsorship acquisition, and community outreach for an 80+ member team with eight vice presidents. I acquired and managed over $20,000 in sponsorships and funding.

I helped the team place in the top 48 of more than 3,600 teams at the FIRST World Championship.
